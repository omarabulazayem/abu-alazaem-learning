# Abu Al-Azaem Brand Identity

## Official palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Target visual share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Calm background | Ivory Mist | `#F7F6F0` | 55% |
| Primary identity & actions | Calm Teal | `#1E6F5C` | 18% |
| Secondary learning accent | Soft Sky Blue | `#4EA8DE` | 12% |
| Text & reading contrast | Deep Charcoal | `#2B2D42` | 10% |
| Rewards & celebration | Warm Gold | `#E9C46A` | 5% |

Warm card surfaces use `#FFFDF8` as a neutral surface derived from the background family. Semantic error red is functional only and is not part of the brand palette.

## Rules

- Do not introduce pink, purple, neon green, or orange as general brand colors.
- Teal is the primary CTA, active state, brand mark and success/progress identity.
- Sky blue is secondary: focus, information, Quran browsing and supportive actions.
- Gold is rare and reserved for stars, rewards, streaks and celebrations.
- Charcoal replaces pure black for normal UI text.
- Large page areas should remain ivory/warm neutral; decorative color should never compete with Quran text or learning tasks.
- Illustration backgrounds must stay low-detail and low-contrast. No readable generated Quranic text should appear inside AI-generated artwork.
- For children 6–11, use larger touch targets and visual hierarchy rather than adding more colors.
